# Northwind BV procurement policy (SUPERSEDED)

Version 3.1, in force from 1 January 2024 until 31 December 2025. Retained for audit of invoices paid in that period. Do not apply to current invoices.

## 3. Spend thresholds and approval authority (2024 to 2025)

- Up to EUR 2,500: the cost centre owner may approve.
- Above EUR 2,500 and up to EUR 15,000: a department head may approve.
- Above EUR 15,000 and up to EUR 75,000: the Finance Director may approve.
- Above EUR 75,000: two board members must approve, and it must be minuted.

## 2. Purchase orders (2024 to 2025)

Every purchase above EUR 1,000 excluding VAT required a purchase order raised before the goods or services were ordered.

## 5. Payment terms (2024 to 2025)

The standard payment term was 45 days from the invoice date.
