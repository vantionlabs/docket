# Delegation of authority matrix

Approval limits by category, in force from 1 January 2026. Read together with the procurement policy, which governs supplier invoices.

## 1. It Hardware

Committed spend on IT hardware up to EUR 500 per order may be approved by the cost centre owner. Above that limit the next authority in the ladder approves. Recurring commitments are assessed on their annual value, not the value of a single invoice.
