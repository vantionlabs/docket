# Capital expenditure approval

Version 1.4. Governs capitalised assets. Operating spend follows the procurement policy.

## 2. Capital approval limits

- Up to EUR 5,000: a department head may approve.
- Above EUR 5,000 and up to EUR 50,000: the Finance Director may approve.
- Above EUR 50,000 and up to EUR 250,000: the board may approve.
- Above EUR 250,000: the board must approve, and it must be minuted.

## 3. Business case

Any capital request above EUR 25,000 requires a written business case with a payback calculation.

## 4. Asset register

Capitalised items are entered in the asset register within 30 days of delivery.
