# Acme Corp Handbook

## Vacation policy
Full-time employees accrue 25 days of paid vacation per year. Unused days
roll over up to a maximum of 10 days into the next calendar year. Vacation
requests must be submitted at least two weeks in advance.

## Remote work
Employees may work remotely up to three days per week. Fully remote
arrangements require director approval and are reviewed annually.

## Expense reimbursement
Business expenses under 50 euro are reimbursed without a receipt. Anything
above 50 euro requires an itemized receipt submitted within 30 days. Travel
is booked through the company portal, not reimbursed after the fact.

## Security
All laptops must use full-disk encryption. Passwords rotate every 90 days.
Production database access requires two-person approval.
